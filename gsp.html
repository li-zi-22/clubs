The Girl Scouts of the Philippines is a values-driven organization that shapes young girls into responsible, confident, and service-oriented leaders. This club actively supports school programs such as Overnight Camping and Barricade Duties during Flag Ceremony. Through these activities, GSP promotes discipline, teamwork, leadership, and a strong sense of community, always guided by the Girl Scout Promise and Law. Laging Handa — to serve, to help in every way, and to build a better tomorrow.
