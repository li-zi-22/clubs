📘 About AnSci ICT Club

The AnSci ICT Club brings together students passionate about information & communication technology. We aim to build skills in coding, networking, web development, and more — turning ideas into tech reality.

Our key focuses:
  • 🖥️ Programming & Web Dev
  • 🌐 Networking & Systems
  • 📱 App / Mobile Dev
