MAPEH CLUB

The MAPEH Club is a school organization in ACNSTHS for students who are passionate about music, arts, and sports. It aims to develop creativity, talent, and teamwork through activities that showcase artistic expression and athletic skills. The club provides opportunities for learners to perform, compete, and collaborate, promoting a fun and dynamic environment that celebrates culture, creativity, and physical excellence.

Activities include:
Sports Tournaments
Poster-Making
Art Exhibits and Contests
Dance Showcases
Music Performances
