📘 About Us

The ACNSTHS Red Cross Youth is a student‑based volunteer group committed to promoting humanitarian values, first aid skills, disaster preparedness, and community service. We strive to make a positive impact through compassion, cooperation, and action.

Our values:
  • 🤝 Empathy & Service
  • 🆘 First Aid & Safety
  • 🌪 Disaster Readiness
  • 💬 Community Engagement

🛠️ Our Missions & Activities


First Aid Training
Teaching lifesaving skills to students & community
Disaster Preparedness Drills
Simulating emergencies & response strategies
Community Outreach
Volunteering in health, welfare & aid initiatives
