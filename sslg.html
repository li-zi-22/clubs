The Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) is the highest governing student body in Antipolo City National Science and Technology Highschool. It serves as the official organization representing the student population, promoting leadership, responsibility, and active participation in school activities. The SSLG organizes activities that foster unity, discipline, and academic excellence while ensuring that every student’s concerns and ideas are heard and addressed.

Activities include:
Leadership Trainings and Seminars
Anti-Bullying and Mental Health Campaigns
Student Assemblies/Forums
Clean-up Drives
